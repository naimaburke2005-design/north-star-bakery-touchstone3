About Our Neighborhood Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: good bread gives people a reason to gather. We grew from baking for neighbors to creating a welcoming stop for busy mornings and everyday celebrations.

Our sourcing philosophy

We value fresh ingredients, thoughtful recipes, and relationships with nearby suppliers. We aim to use seasonal ingredients when available and bake in small batches to keep our selection fresh.
